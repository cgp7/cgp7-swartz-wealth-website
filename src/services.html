How we invest

We mostly use low-cost index funds and ETFs. We don't try to time the market or find the next hot stock. The aim is a portfolio you can stick with when markets fall.

All investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal. Diversification does not guarantee a profit or protect against loss, and index strategies fall with the market as well as rise with it.
