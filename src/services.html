How we invest

Simple, low-cost, and built to last.

We mainly use low-cost index funds and ETFs that track broad markets. We don't try to pick the next hot stock or time the market.

  • Diversified across thousands of companies and many bond issuers.
  • Matched to your goals, timeline and comfort with ups and downs.
  • Rebalanced so your mix doesn't drift.
  • Held at Charles Schwab, in your name.

All investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal. Diversification does not guarantee a profit or protect against loss. Passive strategies follow the market down as well as up.
