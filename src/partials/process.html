How working together goes

Three stages. You'll always know which one you're in and what comes next.

01

Define

The first two weeks

We get the whole picture: accounts, income, taxes, insurance, and what you want the money to do. You send statements and we do the organizing.

02

Strategize

Weeks three to six

We build the plan and walk you through it. Every recommendation comes with the reason behind it and what it costs, in dollars.

03

Realize

From then on

We put the plan in place and manage the investments. We meet at least twice a year, and any time something big changes.
