About us

Independent by design.

Swartz Wealth Management was founded in 2025 to give families and business owners careful, conflict-aware advice, with nothing to sell but the advice itself.

Why we started

Jake Swartz spent the first decade of his career inside companies' finances: working on business sales, analysing investments, and running the budgets and forecasts that executives use to make decisions.

He kept seeing the same gap. Companies get rigorous, numbers-first advice. Families, and the owners who run those companies, often get a sales pitch. Swartz Wealth Management exists to close that gap.

We're an independent registered investment adviser. No bank, broker or insurance company owns us, and we don't have our own funds to sell you.

Where your money lives

We manage your money. We never hold it.

Your accounts are held by Charles Schwab & Co., an independent custodian, in your name. That separation is one of the most important protections you have.

You can always check

Log in to Schwab any time to see your balances, trades and fees. You don't have to take our word for anything.

We can't move money out

Our authority is limited to placing trades and deducting the fee you approved. We can't withdraw your money to ourselves or anyone else.

Compare the numbers

If anything we report ever differs from your Schwab statement, the statement is the record. Tell us and we'll explain.
