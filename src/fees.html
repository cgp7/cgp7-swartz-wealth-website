How billing works

You can see every dollar we're paid.

  1. 01

    You authorize it

    We deduct our fee only with your written permission, given when you open your account.

  2. 02

    Monthly, in advance

    Each month's fee is based on your balance on the last business day of the month before.

  3. 03

    On your statement

    Schwab shows every fee on your statements, which you receive at least quarterly.

Either of us can end the agreement with 30 days' written notice. You'll get a prorated refund of any fee paid for days after it ends.

What our fee doesn't cover

  • Fund expenses. Index funds and ETFs charge their own small annual fees, shown in each fund's prospectus.
  • Custodian and transaction charges. Schwab may charge for some trades, wires or transfers.
  • Insurance premiums, if you buy a policy.

We'll show you these costs before you invest.

Conflicts we want you to know about

  • Insurance commissions. Jake is a licensed insurance agent and may earn commissions on policies he sells. That gives him a reason to recommend insurance. You can buy any policy through any agent you choose.
  • Rollovers. If you move a 401(k) into an account we manage, we earn a fee on it. We'll explain all your options first.
  • Schwab services. Schwab gives advisers like us access to institutional tools and support. Our full Form ADV explains this.
