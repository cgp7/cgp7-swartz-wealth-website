Wealth management

A yearly percentage of the money we manage, with financial planning included. One rate applies to your whole balance, set by the band it falls in.

Example: $2.6 million falls in the 0.85% band, so the fee is $22,100 a year, or $1,841.67 a month. There's no account minimum. Fees may be negotiable, and your advisory agreement sets your final fee.

How billing works

You can see every dollar we're paid.

  1. You approve the fee in writing when you open your account.
  2. We bill monthly, in advance, based on your balance on the last business day of the month before.
  3. Schwab deducts the fee and shows it on your statements, which arrive at least every quarter.
  4. Either of us can end the agreement with 30 days' written notice, and you get back any fee paid for days after it ends.

What our fee doesn't cover

  1. Index funds and ETFs charge their own small yearly fee, shown in each fund's prospectus.
  2. Schwab may charge for some trades, wires or transfers.
  3. Insurance premiums, if you buy a policy, are paid to the insurer.

We'll show you these costs before you invest.

Conflicts you should know about

  1. Jake is a licensed insurance agent and may earn commissions on policies he sells. That gives him a reason to recommend insurance. You can buy any policy through any agent you like.
  2. If you move a 401(k) into an account we manage, we earn a fee on it. We'll explain every option first.
  3. Schwab gives advisers like us institutional tools and support. Our Form ADV explains how.
