How we work

Define. Strategize. Realize.

Three steps, the same for every client. You always know where we are and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Define

    First two weeks

    We learn what you have, what you want and what keeps you up at night. You share statements; we turn them into one clear picture.

  2. 02

    Strategize

    Weeks three to six

    We build your plan and walk you through it in plain English: investments, taxes, insurance, estate, and the order to do things in.

  3. 03

    Realize

    Ongoing

    We put the plan to work, rebalance your portfolio and meet at least twice a year, plus whenever life changes.

As filed, the fee drops at each band edge: $500,000 pays $7,500 but $500,001 pays $6,250; $2.5M pays $25,000 but $2.6M pays $22,100. Tiered billing at the same rates removes the drops but charges more, so the rates would need resetting (and the brochure amending).
