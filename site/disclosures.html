Important disclosures

Swartz Wealth Management LLC (CRD 337684) is an investment adviser registered with the states of Illinois and Michigan. Registration does not imply a certain level of skill or training. The firm offers advisory services only to residents of states where it is registered or exempt from registration.

Information on this website is general education. It is not individualized investment, tax or legal advice, and it is not an offer to buy or sell any security. We give advice only after we sign an advisory agreement with you.

All investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal. Past performance does not guarantee future results, and diversification does not guarantee a profit or protect against loss.

Jacob Swartz is a licensed insurance agent and may receive commissions on insurance products. This is a conflict of interest. You are under no obligation to buy insurance through him.

Examples on this site labelled as example clients are made up. They are not real clients and do not reflect actual results.

Our Form ADV Part 2A brochure describes our services, fees and conflicts in full. It is on the SEC's Investment Adviser Public Disclosure site, or free from us on request.
