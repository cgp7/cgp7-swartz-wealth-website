Who we serve

Three kinds of clients. One standard of care.

Most people who call us are running a business, building a career, or getting ready to retire. Here's what we work on with each, and an example of how it plays out.

Business owners

Your business is probably your biggest asset. Plan around it.

Owners rarely get advice that looks at the company and the household together. We do, alongside your CPA and attorney.

  • Before a sale: what you need from a deal to retire, what you'd keep after taxes, and what to sort out before buyers call.
  • While you run it: paying yourself tax-smartly, and choosing a retirement plan (Solo 401(k), SEP IRA or a company plan) for you and your team.
  • Protecting it: keeping business risk away from family assets, and reviewing buy-sell and key-person coverage.
  • After a sale: investing the proceeds on a plan, not in a rush.

Insurance note: Jake is a licensed insurance agent and may earn commissions. You're never required to buy insurance through us.

Illustration · not a real client

Maria, 56, owns a commercial cleaning company near Grand Rapids.

She has 40 employees and expects offers within five years. She wants to know whether a sale would fund her retirement, and what to do first.

What we'd do: work out the after-tax amount she needs from a sale, set up a retirement plan that helps her and her staff now, coordinate with her CPA and attorney on the deal structure, and map out how to invest the proceeds.

Professionals

Your income is growing. Your plan should keep up.

Rising pay brings more choices and more complexity: stock awards, bonuses, old 401(k)s, a bigger house, college. We help you put it in order.

  • Old 401(k)s: deciding whether to leave, roll over or combine them.
  • Equity and bonuses: how RSUs and deferred pay are taxed, and when to sell.
  • Big goals: a home and your kids' education, saved for at the same time.
  • Investing: low-cost, diversified and tax-aware.
Read: your 401(k) options when you change jobs

Illustration · not a real client

Dev and Priya, 38 and 36, live in Chicago with two kids.

Between them they have four old 401(k)s, a yearly RSU grant and a goal of a bigger house in three years.

What we'd do: combine the old accounts where it lowers cost, set a plan for each RSU grant as it vests, and line up savings for the house and for college.

Families & retirement

Retire on your terms, with a plan your family understands.

The years just before and after retirement hold the biggest one-time decisions most families make. We help you make them in order, with numbers.

  • Income: turning savings into a monthly paycheck.
  • Timing: when to claim Social Security, and pension lump sum or monthly payments.
  • Legacy: beneficiaries, estate documents and gifts, coordinated with your attorney.
  • Organization: one clear picture your spouse or children can follow.

Illustration · not a real client

Tom and Linda, 62 and 59, live near Kalamazoo.

Tom must choose between a pension lump sum and monthly payments. They aren't sure when to claim Social Security.

What we'd do: compare both pension choices in dollars, model different claiming ages for each of them, and build a withdrawal plan that turns their savings into a steady income.
