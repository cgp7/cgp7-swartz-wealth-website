Ask any advisor

Six questions to ask any advisor, with our answers.

Whoever you hire, ask them these. Here's how we'd answer.

Are you a fiduciary?
Yes, when we give investment advice. We're required to put your interests first. Jake is also a licensed insurance agent; when he sells insurance he acts as an agent and may earn a commission. We'll always tell you which role he's in.
How are you paid?
A percentage of the assets we manage (1.50% down to 0.75%), or $150 an hour for planning on its own. Insurance commissions are the only other way we can be paid. See every fee.
Who holds my money?
Charles Schwab, an independent custodian, in an account in your name. We can trade and deduct our fee with your permission. We can't withdraw your money.
What do you invest in?
Mostly low-cost index funds and ETFs that track broad markets. We don't have our own funds to sell you.
Is there a minimum?
No. We work with people at every stage, and the fee schedule is the same for everyone.
Where can I check you out?
On the SEC's public adviser database. Our registration, Form ADV and any disciplinary history (we have none) are listed under CRD 337684.
