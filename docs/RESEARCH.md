## What converts

- **[Kitces Marketing Survey, 2026](https://www.kitces.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Kitces-Marketing-Survey-2026-3.pdf):**
    - A fee schedule on the website had the strongest link to lower acquisition cost.
    - A niche stated on the site was linked to cheaper referrals ($0.06 vs $0.13 per $1 of new revenue).
    - Referrals produce 62% of new clients, and social media had the worst return.
- **[Cerulli/Marketcast via Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2ctaz879nv0iexlnncz5s/ria-intel/the-fight-to-win-unadvised-clients-starts-with-fee-clarity):** 46% of investors without an advisor said unclear costs would be the hardest part of working with one.
- **[Twenty Over Ten](https://samantharussell.twentyoverten.com/editorials/how-does-your-website-compare-to-other-financial-advisors):** a typical advisor site gets about 300 visits a month and converts about 0.75% of them.
- **[Snappy Kraken](https://snappykraken.com/blog/financial-advisor-website-conversion-benchmarks):**
    - Give each page one need and one next step.
    - Say what happens after someone books.
    - Put trust signals next to the call to action.

## Compliance notes (not legal advice)

- The firm is state-registered, so state advertising rules apply.
    - [Illinois 14 Ill. Adm. Code 130.848](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/illinois/Ill-Admin-Code-tit-14-SS-130.848) bans testimonials and limits "free" offers.
    - [Michigan R 451.4.25](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/michigan/Mich-Admin-Code-R-451-4-25) points to the SEC Marketing Rule.
- Footer needs: the states of registration, "registration does not imply a certain level of skill or training", and links to the ADV.
- Individuals are investment adviser representatives, not "Registered Investment Advisors".
- Keep a privacy notice on the site, and archive each published version.
