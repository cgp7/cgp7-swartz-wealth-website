# Brief: which positioning should the new Swartz Wealth Management website lead with?

Mode: quick council, four seats, Rounds 1 and 2 (Round 3 only if scores do not converge). A reversible marketing call.

## THE BRIEF

**Decision:** An outside builder is redesigning the website for Swartz Wealth Management (SWM) and will propose it to the founder, Jake. Which positioning should the homepage lead with?
- **(A) "Both shores" niche:** lead with business owners and professionals in Chicago and West Michigan. Hero line "From the deal table to your kitchen table" uses Jake's M&A background. Lake Michigan map motif. Families still served, shown second.
- **(B) Broad:** families, individuals and business owners as three equal doors. Leads with "Comprehensive. Holistic. Personal.", published fees, and a named process.
- **(C) Keep doing what they do now:** keep the current one-page Squarespace site and only fix the compliance wording.

Both A and B include a published fee schedule with a calculator, a "who holds your money" (Schwab custody) explainer, and guides.

**The business:** state-registered investment adviser (Illinois, Michigan; Texas pending), founded mid-2025, reports $0 assets under management. Two advisors: Jake (Chicago; ex-investment-banking analyst, then FP&A manager) and Adam (Rockford, MI; ex-ultra-high-net-worth family office operations, family-focused). Fees 1.50% under $500K down to 0.75% over $5M, applied to the whole balance; planning $150/hr ($1,500 to $3,000 a plan); no minimum. Passive index funds and ETFs at Schwab. Jake can earn insurance commissions (disclosed). Illinois bans testimonials in adviser ads. The current site is one page with no fees, no location and no content.

**Resources (assumed):** the site costs little cash; Jake and Adam can give 2 to 4 hours a month to content; launch within 6 to 8 weeks.

**What success looks like (assumed):** 3 to 5 booked consultations a month from the site within 6 months, and the first $5M to $10M under management within 12 months.

**Lean:** the builder wants to see both A and B. The builder's earlier plan leaned A. Kitces 2026 survey: a niche stated on the website is linked to cheaper referrals ($0.06 vs $0.13 per $1 of new revenue); a posted fee schedule had the strongest link to lower acquisition cost; 62% of new clients come from referrals; social media had the worst return.
