# Brief: is the drafted Swartz Wealth Management website ready to show Jake?

Mode: full council, all seven seats, three rounds.

## THE BRIEF

**Decision:** An outside builder has drafted a new website for Swartz Wealth Management (SWM) and must decide how to put it in front of the founder, Jake Swartz, this week. Options:
- **(A) Show the full prototype now:** all 10 pages, both homepage versions, yellow review notes on, plus the 41-question list.
- **(B) Show a trimmed version first:** Version A homepage plus the fee page, notes on, and only the 13 starred questions. Hold the rest until Jake answers.
- **(C) Don't show the site yet:** send only the starred questions, wait for Jake's answers and a compliance review, then show the site.

**The site:** see `site-digest.md` (page by page) and the screenshots in `screens/` (open the PNG files). It is tested on phone and desktop with no errors. An earlier quick council recommended Version A (owners first) with conditions.

**The business:** SWM is a state-registered adviser (Illinois, Michigan), founded 2025, with $0 under management and two advisors. The builder is not part of the firm, and Jake has not commissioned the site (assumed from the builder's answers). Payment for the work is not agreed (assumed).

**Resources (assumed):** the builder has evenings and weekends; Jake's time is scarce; no budget is set.

**What success looks like (assumed):** within two weeks, Jake agrees on a direction, answers the starred questions and names a compliance reviewer. Within two months, a version goes live.

**Lean:** the builder has not stated one (assumed). Momentum argues for A; the 41 open questions and unreviewed compliance wording argue for B or C.
